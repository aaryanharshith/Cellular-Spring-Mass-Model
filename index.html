Literature anchors

  • Mitochondrial transport in neurons, about 0.3–1 µm/s (BioNumbers 111658, citing Harris et al., Neuron 2012).
  • Unloaded kinesin and dynein, about 0.8 µm/s (eScholarship mitochondrial motility study).
  • Mitochondria about 0.5 µm across and 1–5 µm long; lysosomes 0.05–0.5 µm across (hippocampus.org).
  • Most peroxisomes 0.1–0.2 µm across (BioNumbers 113108, citing Michels et al., 2005).
  • Lysosome top speed about twice that of lipid droplets and peroxisomes (Valm et al., Nature 2017).
  • No published directional-correlation value for ρ or θc was found. Calibrate against tracked data.
